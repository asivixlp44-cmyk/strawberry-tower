Description
Strawberry Tower is a sweet pink tower obby floating above a sea of clouds. Climb up the inside of a giant studded pink tower through 12 strawberry stages: giant strawberry steps, shortcake stairs, squishy cream puffs, strawberry milk pools and trusses, slippery icing bridges, sliding lego blocks, cream trampolines, gliding sky plates, pumping pistons, melting jelly and spinning strawberry slices, all the way to the giant smiling strawberry at the summit. Every stage is harder than the last, from EASY to INSANE, and every checkpoint pays more Wins the higher you climb. Spend your Wins on the Speed Coil, Gravity Coil, Fusion Coil, the Rainbow Carpet to fly, the Troll Gun to zap your friends, Admin commands (Giant, Tiny, Spin, Sparkles, Rainbow, Fireworks), the Nuke, cute pets (a unicorn, a kitty, a tiger, a puppy, a berry dragon) that multiply your Wins, and sparkly trails. No in-app purchases: everything is earned with Wins. Find the secret golden strawberry in the lobby and race your friends to the top!
